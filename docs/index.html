Say It Backwards

The party game where you hear a word played backwards, copy it, and flip your copy to find out what you really said.

Coming soon on theApp Store 🇬🇧 English · 🇹🇷 Türkçe

How to play

Two players, one phone, lots of laughing. A round takes about a minute.

1

Record a secret word

Player 1 picks a word (or taps 🎲 for a suggestion) and records it without player 2 hearing.

2

Copy it backwards

Player 2 listens to the word played backwards and copies the strange sound as closely as possible.

3

Flip it and guess

The app reverses player 2's copy. Listen, guess the word, then reveal the answer.
