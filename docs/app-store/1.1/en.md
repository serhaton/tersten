## Description (max 4000)

```
Backwards is the party game where your voice goes into reverse.

Player 1 secretly records a word. Player 2 hears it played backwards, copies the strange sound as closely as they can, and records it. Then the app flips player 2's copy the right way round. Does it sound like the real word? Make a guess, reveal the answer and get ready to laugh.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Pick a theme.
2. Player 1 records a secret word. Need an idea? Tap the dice for a suggestion and hold to peek at it.
3. Player 2 listens to the word backwards and copies it.
4. The app reverses the copy. Listen, guess and reveal the answer.
5. Start a new round and swap roles.

SEVEN THEMES
• Footballers
• Halloween
• Celebrities
• Car Brands
• Animals
• Food
• Anything – say whatever you like, and type your secret word if you want it revealed at the end

Every theme has its own colourful wallpaper, its own sound effects and its own word suggestions, nearly 100 in English and more in Turkish.

FEATURES
• Two players, one iPhone or iPad: no accounts, no sign-up
• Instant recording: tap Record and start talking
• English and Turkish, with word suggestions in the language you choose
• Themed sounds, from a referee's whistle to a ghostly "wooo", which you can turn off in Settings
• Your voice stays on your device: recordings are reversed on the device, never uploaded, and deleted when the game ends

Perfect for game nights, family gatherings, road trips and parties.

Backwards is free to play and supported by ads.
```

HOW TO TEST (one person can play both roles)
1. Tap any theme, e.g. "Footballers", and allow microphone access.
2. Player 1 ("Say your secret word"): optionally tap "Suggest a word" and hold "Hold to see". Tap the red microphone button, say a word, tap the button again to stop.
3. Player 2: tap "Listen" to hear the word backwards, as often as you like. Then tap "Record", imitate the sound, tap "Stop".
4. The app reverses the imitation and plays it automatically. "Hear my copy" replays it; "Try again" goes back to step 3.
5. Tap "Show the answer" to hear the original and see the word, then "New game".

MICROPHONE INDICATOR
The orange microphone indicator is on while a Record button is on screen (steps 2 and 3), before it is tapped. This is intended and new in 1.1. The app now starts the recorder and pauses it straight away when a Record button is shown; tapping Record only resumes it. Nothing from before the tap is kept: the fraction of a second captured before the pause is cut from the take, and if the player leaves without recording, the prepared file is deleted. The recorder is released on screens without a Record button, while a full-screen ad is showing, and in the background. While recording, the card says "Recording…" and the button pulses and shows a stop symbol. Recordings are processed only on the device, never uploaded, and deleted when a new game starts or the game screen closes. No background audio.

## App Privacy – how to fill it in (step by step)

The product page currently says "Data Not Collected". That is wrong, because the app shows Google AdMob ads and the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data. The answers below follow Google's own disclosure guide for the SDK (https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure). App Privacy is edited separately from a version: as far as we know the change goes live when you publish it, without waiting for review.

1. App Store Connect › your app › **App Privacy** (left sidebar) › **Edit** next to "Data Collection" (or **Get Started**).
2. "Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?" › **Yes, we collect data from this app** › Next.
3. Tick exactly these seven data types, then Save:
   - Location › **Coarse Location**
   - Identifiers › **Device ID**
   - Usage Data › **Product Interaction**
   - Usage Data › **Advertising Data**
   - Diagnostics › **Crash Data**
   - Diagnostics › **Performance Data**
   - Diagnostics › **Other Diagnostic Data**
4. Each data type now has a "Set Up" link. Answer the same three questions for each one:

   | Data type | Purposes to tick | Linked to the user's identity? | Used for tracking? |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Coarse Location | Third-Party Advertising | Yes | No |
   | Device ID | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | Yes | No |
   | Product Interaction | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | Yes | No |
   | Advertising Data | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | Yes | No |
   | Crash Data | Analytics | No | No |
   | Performance Data | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | Yes | No |
   | Other Diagnostic Data | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | No | No |

5. Check the preview: it should show "Data Linked to You" and "Data Not Linked to You", and no "Data Used to Track You" section.
6. Click **Publish**.
7. Make sure the Privacy Policy URL on the same page is https://serhaton.com/tersten/1.1/en/privacy.html (Turkish: https://serhaton.com/tersten/1.1/tr/privacy.html).

Two of these answers are judgement calls, so check you are comfortable with them:
- **Linked: Yes.** The app has no accounts, but Apple counts data tied to a device identifier as linked, and Google collects a device ID with the ad data. Google calls crash logs "non-user related", hence "No" there.
- **Tracking: No.** The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, never reads the IDFA, and its privacy manifest says it does not track. If you ever add the ATT prompt, these answers must change to Yes.
