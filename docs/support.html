How to play

  1. Pick a theme on the home screen, for example Footballers or Halloween.
  2. Player 1 records a secret word. Tap 🎲 Suggest a word for an idea, and press and hold 👀 Hold to see to read it without player 2 seeing. In Anything, you can type your own word.
  3. Hand the phone to player 2, who taps Play Backwards and listens to the word in reverse.
  4. Player 2 taps Record and copies the backwards sound as closely as possible.
  5. Player 2 taps Listen. The app flips the copy back, so it should sound like the real word. Make a guess!
  6. Tap Show the answer to hear player 1's original recording and see the word.
  7. Tap 🔁 New game to play another round.

Frequently asked questions

The app says it needs the microphone

Backwards needs the microphone to record your voice. If you tapped "Don't Allow", open the iPhone Settings app, scroll down to Backwards and turn on Microphone.

It says my recording is too short

Recordings need to be at least a quarter of a second long. Tap Record, say the word clearly, and tap Stop once you've finished speaking.

I can't hear anything

Turn up the volume with the buttons on the side of your iPhone. The game plays sound even when the silent switch is on. If you use Bluetooth headphones, check they are connected.

How do I change the language?

Tap the colourful ⚙ button in the top-right corner and choose Türkçe or English. Word suggestions switch to the language you pick.

How do I turn off the button sounds?

Open ⚙ Settings and switch off App sounds. Your recordings still play as normal.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the game free. There is a small banner at the bottom of the game screen and a full-screen ad after every second finished round, shown when you start the next round.

Are my recordings saved or shared?

No. Recordings stay on your iPhone, are only used for the current round, and are deleted when you start a new game or leave the game screen. See the Privacy Policy.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Backwards is currently available for iPhone.
