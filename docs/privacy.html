Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: your voice recordings never leave your iPhone. We don't have accounts and we don't collect personal information ourselves. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to deliver them.

This policy explains how the Say It Backwards app (called "Tersten" in Turkish; "the app", "we") handles information when you use it on iPhone.

1. Microphone and voice recordings

  • The app asks for microphone access so you can record words during a game.
  • Recordings are processed only on your device: the app reverses them and plays them back.
  • Recordings are saved temporarily in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are deleted when you start a new game or leave the game screen.
  • Recordings are never uploaded, shared, or sent to us or anyone else.
  • You can turn off microphone access at any time in iPhone Settings › Backwards › Microphone. The game can't record without it.

2. Words you type

In the Anything theme you can type your secret word. It is kept only in the app's memory for the current round and is never saved or sent anywhere.

3. Settings

Your language choice and whether app sounds are on are saved on your device so the app remembers them. This information never leaves your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the game screen and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (for example, your country);
  • advertising and app-instance identifiers;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

The app doesn't ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) isn't shared with Google. Google may still use the data above to provide, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

When you first open the app, you are asked for consent using Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can choose to give or refuse consent. To change your choice later, delete and reinstall the app, and you'll be asked again.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, a name or an email address.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't use analytics tools of our own.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience party game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests relate to Google's advertising data. You can manage them through Google's tools at My Ad Center. You can also contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
