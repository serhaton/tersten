Say It Backwards

The party game where you hear a word played backwards, copy it, and flip your copy to find out what you really said.

Download on theApp Store 🇬🇧 English · 🇹🇷 Türkçe

How to play

Two players, one iPhone or iPad, lots of laughing. A round takes about a minute, and a game is six rounds.

1

Record a word

One player picks a word (or taps Suggest a word for an idea) and records it without the other hearing.

2

Copy it backwards

The other player taps Listen to hear the word played backwards, as often as needed and slowed down if it helps, then taps Record and copies the strange sound.

3

Flip it and guess

The app reverses the copy and plays it straight away. Guess the word, reveal the answer, and score a point if you got it. Then the players swap roles.

🔒

Your voice stays on your device

Recordings are reversed on the device and deleted when the game ends. Nothing is uploaded.

English and Turkish

Themed sounds

⚡

Instant recording

Recording starts the moment you tap, so the start of your word is never cut off.
